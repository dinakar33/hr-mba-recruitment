Academic Modules & Curriculum Mapping

Detailed analysis of how this platform bridges postgraduate classroom theory with real-world HR technology execution.

1. Talent Acquisition & ATS Analytics

The Stage 1 ATS Audit demonstrates the foundational mechanics of automated resume screening used by Fortune 500 companies. It actively explores:

  • Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF): How algorithms weight specific corporate keywords against general vocabulary to determine candidate relevance.
  • Algorithmic Bias & Formatting Constraints: Demonstrating how complex PDF layouts (tables, dual columns) fail parsing engines, leading to false negatives in the recruitment funnel.
  • Boolean Search Logic: How HR professionals use AND/OR/NOT operators to build the very keyword arrays this system scans against.

2. Organizational Behavior (OB)

The Initial Archetype Assessment and Virtual GD are direct applications of core OB theories regarding human interaction in corporate structures:

  • Tuckman's Stages of Group Development: The Virtual GD forces candidates to navigate Forming, Storming, and Norming phases rapidly with distinct AI personality types (Analytical, Creative, Pragmatic).
  • Behavioral Profiling: The archetype generation mirrors standard corporate psychometric tools (like DiSC or MBTI) to predict conflict resolution styles.
  • Cognitive Biases: The platform highlights the dangers of the Halo Effect and Confirmation Bias by strictly separating the automated quantitative scoring from the Admin's subjective qualitative override.

3. Strategic HRM (SHRM)

The Stage 4 AI Interview is built strictly upon SHRM best practices for predictive candidate success:

  • The STAR Method Validation: Questions are explicitly designed to force Situation, Task, Action, and Result formatting, the gold standard for behavioral consistency validation.
  • Predictive Validity: Structuring the interview to measure competencies (resilience under tight time budgets, stakeholder influence) rather than just technical skill.
  • Strategic Alignment: Assessing if a candidate's core philosophy prioritizes long-term employee well-being alongside aggressive quarterly corporate metrics.

4. Industrial Relations & Labor Law

The Labor Rights Module provides critical statutory literacy necessary for ethical HR management and personal career protection:

  • The POSH Act 2013: Understanding the legal mandates for Internal Complaints Committees (ICC) and the definitions of safe workplace environments.
  • CTC Structuring & Taxation: Breaking down Base Pay versus Variable Pay, Gratuity eligibility, and Provident Fund (EPF) statutory contributions.
  • Severance & Notice Periods: The legal enforceability of non-compete clauses, notice period buyouts, and termination severance under current labor codes.

5. HR Information Systems (HRIS) & Analytics

The Cloud-Synced Backend and Executive Passport function as a live demonstration of a modern HRIS architecture:

  • Real-Time Data Streaming: How NoSQL cloud databases (Firebase) instantly push candidate metadata to administrative dashboards without page refreshes.
  • Quantitative Readiness Indexing: Aggregating disparate data points (ATS scores, cognitive agility, behavioral sentiment) into a single, actionable executive metric.
  • Automated Reporting: Utilizing API hooks to generate and dispatch formatted corporate dossiers directly to candidate email systems (Gmail integration).
