Employee Rights & Literacy

Essential regulatory knowledge every professional must master.

1. Offer Letter Fundamentals

Always examine your Cost to Company (CTC) breakdown. Base salary determines your provident fund and gratuity calculations, whereas variable pay is contingent on individual and company performance metrics. Do not let inflated variable components lower your in-hand expectations.

2. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

Familiarize yourself with the 2013 Act and the Vishakha Guidelines. It is mandatory for corporations to have an active Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). You have an absolute right to a safe, non-hostile workplace environment.

3. Working Hours & Notice Periods

Understand statutory working hour caps under the New Labour Codes, eligibility for overtime, and the legal enforceability of non-compete clauses and notice period buyouts.
