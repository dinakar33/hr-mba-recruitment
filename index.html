Labor Rights & Compliance

1. Offer Letter Fundamentals (CTC vs. Base)
Cost to Company (CTC) is a company's total expense on you, not your take-home salary. It includes Base Pay, Variable Pay/Bonuses (which are not guaranteed), Provident Fund (EPF) contributions, and Gratuity. Always negotiate based on the fixed Base Salary, as that determines your actual monthly deposit and retirement benefits.

2. POSH Act (Prevention of Sexual Harassment)
Mandatory under the 2013 Act, any organization with 10+ employees must have an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). Sexual harassment includes unwelcome physical contact, demands for sexual favors, or sexually colored remarks. You have the legal right to a safe workplace and retaliation against complainants is strictly prohibited.

3. Employment Contracts & Notice Periods
Notice periods are legally binding. If you leave early, the company can demand a "notice buyout" (you pay them the salary for unserved days). Additionally, non-compete clauses are common but often legally unenforceable in Indian courts if they restrict your fundamental right to earn a livelihood after resignation.
