Labor Rights & Corporate Compliance

Essential statutory knowledge every professional must master before signing an offer letter.

CTC vs. In-Hand Salary

Cost to Company (CTC) is the total amount the company spends on you, including hidden costs. Base Salary is the fixed component. Variable Pay is contingent on performance. Never confuse CTC with your monthly take-home pay. Provident Fund (EPF) and Gratuity are deducted from your Base.

POSH Act Compliance

The Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act mandates that any organization with 10 or more employees must constitute an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). You have the statutory right to a safe workplace, and HR is legally obligated to address written complaints within strict timelines.

Employment Contracts

Always review Notice Periods and Non-Compete Clauses. While non-competes are often standard, courts generally favor the employee's right to earn a livelihood post-termination, making overly restrictive non-competes difficult for employers to enforce legally.
